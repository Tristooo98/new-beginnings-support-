Mental Wellness Support & Referrals

We are currently determining the mental health services we will offer. End-of-Life Support

Practical guidance, compassionate listening, and help organizing questions, wishes, and next steps with loved ones.

✿

Funeral Needs

Support with understanding options, preparing questions, and connecting with licensed funeral providers where needed. Funeral arrangements are completed by the appropriate provider.

◇

Buying & Selling Items

Discuss the sale or purchase of household belongings and personal items before or after a death, with clear communication and respectful handling.

▤

Collections & Valuables

We can discuss collectibles such as trading cards, comics, memorabilia, and other collections, including options for evaluating or selling items.

A clear, respectful process

Mental health service model: The exact service model is still being decided. Update this section once the service model, provider, qualifications, and scope are confirmed.

Start with a conversation. You can explain what you need, ask questions, and decide what feels right for you.

1. Reach out

Call or send an appointment request with the best way to contact you.

2. Talk through your needs

We’ll discuss your situation, what support is available, and any fees or limits before proceeding.

3. Agree on next steps

For mental health concerns, we can discuss appropriate support options and referrals. Clinical therapy, legal, estate, and funeral services should be provided by appropriately qualified professionals where required.

Important: This website is an introductory service page, not a crisis service or a substitute for licensed clinical, legal, financial, or funeral-provider advice. Please confirm the provider's qualifications and service scope before booking. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911 in Canada. If you are thinking about suicide, call or text 988 in Canada.
